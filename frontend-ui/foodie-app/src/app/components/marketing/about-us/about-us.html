About Us

Good food, from the kitchens around the corner

Foodie Inc connects people with the restaurants already in their neighbourhood, and gets the food to them while it is still worth eating. We started in Cape Town and we are building outwards.

What we care about

  • Local first

    Independent kitchens sit alongside the big names, and both are found the same way: by what is near you and open now.

  • Fair to couriers

    Drivers choose their own hours, are never double-booked, and keep their tips in full.

  • Honest with restaurants

    Owners control their own menu, prices and hours. Orders are paid before they reach the kitchen.

  • Straightforward for customers

    Clear delivery fees, live order tracking, and a notification when the driver is close.
