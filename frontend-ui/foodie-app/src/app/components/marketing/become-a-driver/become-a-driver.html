What to expect

  • Nearest orders first

    Dispatch offers you the closest pickup to where you actually are, so less of your time is spent driving empty.

  • One order at a time

    You are never double-booked. An order is held for you the moment it is assigned and released only when it is done.

  • Keep your tips

    Whatever a customer adds as a tip goes to you in full.

  • Go online when you want

    Mark yourself available from your delivery worklist to start receiving offers. Go offline just as easily.

What you will need

  • A vehicle

    Car, scooter or motorbike, with a valid licence and registration in your name.

  • A smartphone

    Any recent Android or iPhone. Your delivery worklist runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install.

  • The right to work

    A South African ID or a valid work permit.

How to get started

  1. Create your Foodie Inc account.
  2. Our team verifies your documents and enables courier access.
  3. Add your vehicle type and registration, then go online and start accepting deliveries.
